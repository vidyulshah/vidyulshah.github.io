About

Intelligence that has to survive contact with real hardware.

I deploy intelligent systems on NVIDIA Jetson platforms inside embedded Linux environments, where timing, thermals and a noisy bus matter as much as the algorithm. Most of my work sits at the seam between software that plans and hardware that moves.

What I bring is full hardware–software ownership. I write the ROS2 nodes, flash the board, debug the peripherals over SPI, I²C and CAN, and have machined the physical parts those systems control — including CNC-fabricated humanoid skeletal frames and vacuum-poured silicone dermis.

That range comes from working across humanoid autonomy at Indus Humanoid Lab, UAV flight control at Yottec Systems, and legacy avionics modernisation at Alchemy Techsol. I hold an MSc in Robotics from Heriot-Watt University in Edinburgh, with a paper published at ACM ICMI 2021.

Open to relocation: Germany · Netherlands · Singapore · United Kingdom · Australia. Remote roles considered globally.
