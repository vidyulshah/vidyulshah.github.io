About Me

Building intelligence
for the physical world.

I build autonomous robotic systems — from ROS2 architecture down to the physical hardware that executes it. With 4+ years of experience, I specialise in deploying intelligent systems on NVIDIA Jetson platforms (Nano, Xavier, Orin) within embedded Linux environments.

What sets me apart is full hardware-software ownership: I write the ROS2 nodes, flash the Jetson, debug the peripherals (SPI/I²C/CAN), and have machined the physical parts those systems control — including CNC fabrication of humanoid skeletal frames and vacuum-pour silicone dermis casting.

My work spans humanoid robot autonomy at Indus Humanoid Lab, UAV flight-control systems at Yottec Systems, and legacy avionics modernisation at Alchemy Techsol. I hold an MSc in Robotics from Heriot-Watt University (Edinburgh, UK) and have a published paper at ACM ICMI 2021.
